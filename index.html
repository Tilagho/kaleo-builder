How to use Kaleo Builder

All sizes are real (actual) dimensions: a 2x4 is 1-1/2" x 3-1/2". Type lengths like 7'6 1/2, 90.5 or 3-1/2.

Add a part
Click it in the library, or drag it onto the ground. Pick the lumber length and material first.
Move
Drag a part along the ground. Use the colored gizmo arrows to move along X / Y (up) / Z. Parts snap to the grid and to the faces of other parts. Hold Shift to skip snapping.
Orbit / pan / zoom
Drag empty space to orbit, right-drag (or two fingers) to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
On a phone
One finger orbits, even if you start on a part. Pinch zooms. Two fingers pan. Tap a part to select it. Then use Slide or Left / Right / Back / Front / Up / Down. Parts and Details open as sheets so the model stays on screen. Drag a library part up onto the view to drop it there, or tap it to add it.
Cuts & joinery
Select a board, then use Cuts & Joinery in the Inspector: notch, dado, rabbet, half-lap, miter/bevel, mortise, tenon, hole. Sheet goods also get a Freehand profile (e.g. a surfboard).
R / Shift+R
Turn the selected part around the vertical axis / tip it over (around X)
T
Tip around Z (front-to-back axis)
Delete
Delete selected part
Ctrl+D
Duplicate (offset by the grid, or by the part size)
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y
Undo / redo
Arrow keys
Nudge by one grid step (Shift+↑/↓ moves up and down)
G E M
Move gizmo, rotate gizmo, measure tool
F
Zoom to fit
Esc
Deselect / clear measurements

Your work autosaves in this browser. Use Save to keep a .json file you can open later or on another device. Prices are rough Maui estimates (mainland Home Depot x 1.3) — edit them on the Prices tab.

Freehand profile

Click to add points along the outline (top half when mirroring). Drag points to adjust. The curve is a smooth spline through your points. Grid is 1".
